Real-time validation

Errors and inconsistencies are flagged during data entry via an underline in the editor, a marker in the margin, and an explicit message. The user can make corrections immediately, without waiting for the match to end.

When an error is detected, a red cross appears in the editor’s margin; hovering the mouse cursor over it displays a list of the errors on that line. Red marks located on the right within the editor’s ruler indicate the positions of errors throughout the entire document, allowing you to navigate directly to them with a single click.
