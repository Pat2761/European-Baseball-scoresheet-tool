Syntax highlighting

The various elements of the input (keywords, names, numbers, comments, etc.) are displayed in distinct colors. The text becomes easier to read, and typos are more easily spotted.

In the match editor, each type of input element is displayed in a different color. This coloring is applied automatically as you type. It makes the text easier to read and allows you to spot input errors at a glance: a word that does not appear in the expected color is often misspelled or misplaced. The colors used can be customized in the tool’s preferences.

The following elements are distinguished:

  • Keywords (in purple): these are reserved words in the scoring language that describe match actions. They are recognized by the tool, and their color confirms that they are written correctly.
  • Proper nouns (in blue): these are the names of teams and players. They are thus distinguished from keywords and the rest of the input.
  • The numbers (in gray): for example, player numbers or numerical values ​​related to an action—stand out immediately within the text.
  • Comments (in gray) are notes that users add for their own reference. They are displayed discreetly, as they are not factored into the scoring.
  • Outs (in red): You can count the number of outs in an inning more quickly.
